**Selected Companies:**
1. **Revolut**
   - Chosen for its positioning as a leading player in the fintech sector with a remarkable 95% revenue increase in 2023, indicating strong growth and sustained demand for its digital banking solutions.

2. **ASML Holding**
   - Selected due to its critical role in the semiconductor industry, with a 30% sales growth in 2023, and expectations for continued earnings uplifts due to the rising demand for advanced chip manufacturing technologies.

**Companies Not Selected:**
1. **Klarna**
   - Although it shows strong growth in the BNPL market, its potential investment risks due to market sensitivity compared to fintech and semiconductor industries lead to its exclusion.

2. **CD Projekt**
   - Despite its well-established brand and upcoming releases, increased competition and high expectations around future performance create uncertainty about its investment potential.

3. **Adyen**
   - While it has a strong market presence, its revenue growth is solid but does not match the rapid expansion seen by Revolut and ASML, leading to its exclusion from the final selection.
